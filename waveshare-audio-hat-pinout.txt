Audio codec chip: PCM5122
Control interface: I2C (I2C1 0x4C)
Audio interface: I2S
Firmware: I2C (I2C0 0x50)

The board contains a flash EEPROM for holding firmware when working with Raspberry Pi OS (I2C0 0x50).
